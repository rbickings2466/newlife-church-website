I. Policy Statement
This policy outlines the procedures for the reporting and investigation of suspected child abuse and neglect. New Life Bible Fellowship Church is committed to protecting all children from harm. All church employees, volunteers, and contractors are mandated reporters under Delaware law and must report any reasonable suspicion of child abuse or neglect. Failure to report may result in disciplinary action and legal penalties.
________________


II. Definitions
* Child Abuse: Any act or omission by a parent, guardian, or other person responsible for a child's care, custody, or welfare that results in physical injury, sexual abuse, emotional abuse, or mental injury to a child.
* Child Neglect: The failure to provide a child with adequate food, clothing, shelter, medical care, or supervision.
* Mandated Reporter: An individual required by law to report suspected cases of child abuse or neglect. In Delaware, this includes all church employees, volunteers, and contractors.
* DFS: The Delaware Division of Family Services, the state agency responsible for investigating child abuse and neglect reports.
________________


III. Reporting Procedures
* Immediate Reporting: Any employee, volunteer, or contractor who has reasonable suspicion that a child is being abused or neglected must immediately report their concerns.
* To Whom to Report:
   1. Internal Reporting: Report concerns to a church leader (e.g., pastor, elder, or children's ministry director). The church leader will then report the suspected abuse to the appropriate state agency.
   2. External Reporting (Direct): While internal reporting is encouraged, the individual with the suspicion can and should make a direct report to the Delaware Division of Family Services (DFS) by calling the Child Abuse and Neglect Report Line at 1-800-292-9582.
* Documentation: The person making the report should document all observed facts and statements that led to the suspicion. This documentation should be factual and objective, avoiding personal opinions or assumptions.
* Confidentiality: All reports and related information are confidential. The identity of the reporter will be protected to the extent permitted by law.
________________
IV. Church Responsibilities
* Training: The church will provide annual training to all employees and volunteers on how to identify and report suspected child abuse and neglect.  
* Cooperation: The church will cooperate fully with DFS and law enforcement agencies during any investigation.
* Non-Retaliation: The church will not take any retaliatory action against an employee, volunteer, or contractor who, in good faith, reports a suspected case of child abuse or neglect.
________________


V. Employee Code of Conduct and Background Checks
* All employees, volunteers, and contractors who work with children are subject to criminal background checks as a condition of employment or service.
* Any employee, volunteer, or contractor who is a subject of an investigation for child abuse or neglect will be placed on administrative leave pending the outcome of the investigation.
* Any employee, volunteer, or contractor found to have committed child abuse or neglect will be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination, and may face legal prosecution.
